6. THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES:
Across India's urban rental ecosystem, over Rs.40,000 Crore in tenant security deposits is trapped in opaque, dispute-prone handoffs. Landlords insert predatory >2-month deposit or blanket painting-forfeiture clauses before move-in, while tenants routinely lose 20%-50% of their deposit at move-out without tamper-proof baseline records or neutral arbitration. Meanwhile, landlords lack instant protection against duplicate photo re-uploads or selfie spoofing, and both parties lack automated legal closure records.

RentShield solves this end-to-end with a 6-Step Cryptographic & AI Cyber-Escrow Protocol:
1. MTA 2021 Smart Lease & Deposit Risk Scanner (Pre-Lease Shield): Powered by Google Gemini 3.8 Flash (POST /api/tenancy/audit-lease), checks rental clauses against Indian Model Tenancy Act (MTA) 2021 Sec 11 (2-month residential cap), Sec 13/15 (normal wear-and-tear protection), and Sec 21 (eviction notice), scoring 0-100 risk and generating copyable legal replacement clauses.
2. Local WebCrypto SHA-256 Sealing (Step 01 & 02): Move-in photos are fingerprinted inside the browser via window.crypto.subtle before landlord revision/lock — making post-hoc pixel edits mathematically detectable.
3. Real-World Two-Party QR Escrow Terminals (Step 03 & Step 05): The Receiver's screen (#owner-receiving-terminal in Stage 1, #guest-refund-terminal in Stage 2) displays a live NPCI UPI QR matrix while the Payer gets Razorpay Verified Escrow + Direct UPI with a strict zero self-approval gate — auto-clearing both dashboards to Rs.0 on completion.
4. Triple Anti-Fraud Engine + 1-Click Judge Evidence Chips (Step 04): Blocks duplicate move-in photos via SHA-256 hash checks, blocks human selfies/UI screenshots via a 32x32 spatial skin-cluster & SSIM guard, and invokes Gemini 3.8 Flash under MTA 2021 Sec 13/15 to compute fair deductions vs. normal wear-and-tear.
5. Statutory Closure & Zero-Server /verify Courtroom (Step 06): Generates a scannable Society Move-Out Gate Pass & NOC, an accounting-ready Official Escrow Settlement Receipt, and lets any arbitrator verify photo bytes 100% offline in-browser.

7. CHALLENGES I RAN INTO:
- Backwards Real-World QR Payment UX: Early prototypes rendered payment QRs on the payer's own popup and permitted self-approval. Re-architecting PaymentModal + Receiver QR Terminals separated Payer Checkout from Receiver QRs with strict counterparty confirmation (DEPOSIT_CLAIMED / REFUND_CLAIMED) and <200ms Razorpay verification.
- Defeating Photo Spoofing Pre-Upload: Built a 32x32 spatial skin-cluster & chrominance heuristic calibrated on real beige interiors vs. portraits alongside SHA-256 byte-hash matching to stop fake submissions client-side before hitting Gemini Vision.
- 2026 Gemini Model Deprecations & TTS RIFF Framing: Implemented dynamic /v1beta/models discovery plus RIFF-safe WAV parsing in our Gemini Puck TTS pipeline to eliminate double-header pops and IPTC XML tail noise.
